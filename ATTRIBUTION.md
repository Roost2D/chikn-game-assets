Existing Chikn, Roostr and FarmLand pack files remain owned by Chikn and retain the Chikn Community Asset Pack Non-Commercial Licence, Version 1.1. Newly imported official Coqfight files and their native derivatives retain the separately supplied Chikn Community Asset Licence. Their rights records identify its exact document and hash; the runtime archive preserves it under `coqfight-v1/LICENSE.md`.

When redistributing, include the unmodified `CHIKN-COMMUNITY-ASSET-LICENSE_PUBLIC.md` and the applicable Asset Manifest. Modified assets must be identified as modified and unofficial as required by the licence.

The repository does not grant commercial permission or claim ownership of protected visual or audio content.

## Independently authored project material

Independently authored project rigs, animation timing, build tools, manifests, and other project metadata are separately licensed under Apache-2.0. This excludes imported official Coqfight skeletons, transforms, attachments and animation data, including native derivatives. The published `water_swim_ring_coq.png` is Roost2D project artwork under Apache-2.0. Artwork displayed by either rig set remains governed by its source classification.

Unrelated project files such as `eggorithm.png` are listed under `excludedPaths` and do not enter published source or runtime artifacts.

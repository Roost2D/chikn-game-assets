# Commercial use

The official Chikn, Roostr and FarmLand visual assets classified as `CHIKN-COMMUNITY-NONCOMMERCIAL` are provided for community non-commercial use under the Chikn team's existing community permission.

This repository does not grant commercial permission.

Before commercially distributing, monetizing, advertising or using the protected visual assets in a commercial service, obtain a separate agreement directly from the Chikn team through its official community or business channels.

The commercial restriction does not apply to entries explicitly classified as Apache-2.0, including software, schemas, generic tooling, independently authored project metadata, and `water_swim_ring_coq.png`, when used without protected Chikn visual assets.
